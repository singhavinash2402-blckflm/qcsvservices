# QCSV Visual Asset Register

**Document type:** Internal design-planning document  
**Purpose:** Track planned visual assets, source research and rights review before implementation.  
**Status:** Initial planning register; no assets have been downloaded, licensed or approved.

## Status Values

- **Planned:** Asset need is identified; no source has been selected.
- **Researching:** Candidate sources are being evaluated.
- **Source identified:** A candidate source is recorded, but license and rights review are incomplete.
- **Rights review required:** Source and license details need verification before use.
- **Client review required:** QCSV approval is needed because the asset may imply QCSV-specific people, clients, facilities, technology or work.
- **Approved for implementation:** Source, rights, usage, treatment and required approvals have been verified and recorded.
- **Rejected:** Asset or source should not be used.

No asset may be marked **Licensed**, **Approved for implementation** or equivalent until the actual source and applicable rights have been verified.

## QCSV Visual Source Strategy

The primary QCSV visual identity must combine:

**Technology / IT Services**  
**+ Validation / Quality**  
**+ Regulated-industry credibility**

QCSV must not visually appear to be primarily a manufacturing company. Technology and enterprise-system imagery should lead the visual language, with validation, quality and regulated-industry context providing relevance and credibility.

For core identity assets, original or generated QCSV visuals are preferred to generic stock imagery. They allow QCSV to establish a distinctive technology-led visual identity without implying a client, facility, partnership or unsupported capability. Any generated visual still requires an internal content, visual, technical and rights review before implementation.

## Recommended QCSV Visual Direction

- **Homepage:** Technology and enterprise-system imagery should dominate. The current client set does not contain a suitable technology-led homepage hero; do not use the audit stamp, project diagrams, quality graphics, SAP logo panel or orange project-management banner as a substitute.
- **Industry pages:** Reserve manufacturing, laboratory, quality and process visuals for relevant industry pages. The current set has no clearly suitable, high-quality industry-specific Pharmaceuticals, Medical Devices, Life Sciences, Semiconductor or Regulated Manufacturing photograph; do not repurpose generic diagrams as industry evidence.
- **Service pages:** `QA_5.jpg` may support Quality Assurance or Validation & Quality as a secondary visual after rights, embedded wording and claim implications are reviewed. `Picture3-1.jpg`, `quality-assurance-1.png` and the project-methodology graphics may support a limited methodology/process context, but their small size or text-heavy design prevents primary use. `SAP-1.jpg` is limited to SAP Services and requires trademark and scope review.
- **Project Delivery:** `PM_New-1.jpg` and one verified project-methodology graphic may support Project Management only as secondary visuals, subject to the embedded text and low-resolution limitations.
- **Success Stories:** Prefer an abstract or technical visual system rather than fabricated project photography. None of the current client images should imply a specific confirmed project, customer, outcome or technology beyond what is explicitly approved.
- **About:** The current set does not provide a suitable neutral About/QCSV visual. Do not use generic stock-style quality imagery as a substitute for company history, people, partnerships or facilities.
- **Do not use:** `Audit-logo.jpg` should not be used. `projectMethodology-1-300x300.png` should not be used in addition to its apparent duplicate C04. Avoid all images as homepage-default visuals where embedded text, third-party marks, low resolution or generic symbolism would weaken the technology/IT-services-led identity.
- **Resolution and cropping:** C03, C04, C05, C07 and C08 are low-resolution or text-heavy for prominent responsive use. C01, C02, C06 and C08 have usable landscape ratios, but ratio alone does not make them suitable. All candidates require responsive crop testing, text-legibility review and rights confirmation before implementation.

## Source And Rights Review Checklist

For each candidate asset, record:

- The direct source URL, not only a search-result URL.
- Creator or author name.
- Current license name and license URL.
- Whether commercial use, modification and web publication are permitted.
- Attribution requirements and the exact attribution text if required.
- Download date and original filename.
- Any model, property, trademark or recognizable-person considerations.
- Crop, color, overlay, compositing or generated-image modifications.
- QCSV approval where the visual could imply a specific client, facility, project, partnership or capability.

Potential source categories include Pexels, Unsplash, client-owned imagery, original QCSV photography, and original/generated imagery. These are candidate categories only; none of the register entries is licensed or approved until the actual source and rights are verified.

Competitor websites, including Artixio, NNIT, QBridge or other competitor sources, must not be used as image sources or copied as visual direction.
