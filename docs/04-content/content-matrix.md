# QCSV Website Content Matrix

**Document type:** Internal planning document  
**Purpose:** Source of truth for planning the About, Industries and Success Stories website areas.  
**Status:** Working matrix; all unsupported QCSV claims require client confirmation before publication.

## How To Read This Matrix

- **Confirmed QCSV capability** means the wording is supported by the current QCSV project material.
- **General industry concept/context** is background terminology for planning only. It is not evidence of QCSV capability.
- **Client confirmation required** identifies information that must be supplied or approved by QCSV before publication.
- Public copy must not imply certifications, awards, customer relationships, approvals, quantitative outcomes or guarantees without approved evidence.

## 4. Content Governance

### Source Hierarchy

1. Client-provided QCSV information is the primary source for capabilities, services, industries, success stories, roles, locations, people and outcomes.
2. Authoritative external sources validate general industry terminology and context only. Examples include FDA and ICH for pharmaceutical quality context, FDA and official ISO-related material for medical-device quality context, and recognized life-sciences or manufacturing sources where relevant.
3. External research must not be used to imply that QCSV follows a regulation, standard, framework or certification unless QCSV confirms it.
4. Competitor websites, including Artixio, NNIT, QBridge or other websites, must never be copied or used as a source for QCSV positioning, structure, claims or wording.

### Claim Classification

- **Confirmed QCSV capability:** Traceable to current client-provided or explicitly approved QCSV material.
- **General industry concept/context:** Neutral background language that explains an industry or service area without claiming QCSV experience.
- **Client confirmation required:** Any unsupported QCSV-specific detail, including methods, roles, technologies, clients, locations, outcomes, credentials or delivery scope.

### Publication Rules

- All public-facing copy must use original QCSV wording.
- Do not invent founding dates, employee counts, offices, clients, partnerships, certifications, awards, approvals, metrics, timelines, team sizes, technologies, regulatory outcomes or guarantees.
- Success stories may show only confirmed title, geography and industry until QCSV approves challenge, approach or outcome details.
- Industry pages must not imply QCSV certification, regulatory approval, compliance guarantees or specific customer relationships.
- Industry and regulatory research must be reviewed for currency and summarized rather than copied.

### Review Before Publication

1. Map each factual claim to approved QCSV material.
2. Mark general research context separately from QCSV capability.
3. Mark unsupported details as **Client confirmation required**.
4. Check all success-story fields for invented challenge, approach or outcome language.
5. Review regulatory and standards wording for implied certification or compliance claims.
6. Complete client approval, editorial, accessibility, link and technical review before publication.
